Neural networks and Transformers, built from scratch · v2

From arithmetic to a working language model

A free, self-contained course in nine parts. No calculus, linear algebra, or ML background assumed — just arithmetic, and roughly two hours a part. Every idea is built before it's used, checked against small numbers you can verify by hand, and made checkable through a live widget rather than just described.

All nine parts, in order
Part 0

Big Picture

What a neural network, an LLM, and attention actually are — in plain language, with two toy animations, no maths yet.
Part 1

Foundations

Functions, vectors, matrices, exponentials, derivatives, the chain rule, the gradient, and probability — the plain maths every later part leans on.
Part 2

PyTorch

Illustrated

Part 8

References

The papers and explainers this course draws on — where each idea originally came from, or where it's explained a second way.
How this course works
Every widget makes one claim checkable

A slider or a button exists to let you verify a specific stated consequence — not to decorate the page. Drag the gradient widget's direction handle and watch the rate of change peak exactly when it aligns with the gradient; set a learning rate too high and watch the loss visibly diverge. If a figure doesn't let you check something, it's been cut.

Generic maths first, network maths second

Part 1 never mentions a neural network. Every tool it builds — a dot product, a derivative, a probability — gets used for the first time in Part 3, so that by the time a formula shows up in later parts, nothing in it is unfamiliar.
